TGH Shorts Studio

A local, no-login tool that turns your guides (or any RSS/URL/topic) into a narrated, captioned short-form video — built from real code/screenshots and generated visuals, never AI-generated imagery. Runs entirely on this computer; nothing is sent anywhere except a service you've explicitly given a key for.

This is a custom build made for you, not a published app with a live updater — there's nothing to "check for updates" against. New versions arrive as a new zip; extract it over this folder to update (your data/config.json and Library are kept, since they live outside app/).
