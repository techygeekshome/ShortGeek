What it does

Takes one of your guides, any RSS feed or URL, or a bare topic, and builds a narrated, captioned 1080x1920 short from it. The script, the voice, the captions and the visuals are all produced on this computer.

What it will not do

No account and no sign-up. It never uploads your videos and it never posts on your behalf. There is no telemetry. It makes no outbound call at all unless you press Check for updates, or you have explicitly configured a voice or AI key of your own, and then only for that feature.

Support

ShortGeek

Something not working

Open an issue on GitHub. Say what source you used, what you expected and what you got. Issues are read by a person, not a bot.

The voice failed, or sounds robotic

The free Edge voice uses an unofficial Microsoft endpoint that occasionally rejects a request. Install the offline fallback with winget install eSpeak-NG.eSpeak-NG and you will always have a working voice, even when Edge has a bad day.

Rendering fails

Check the queue panel for the error text. Almost every render failure is either a source that returned no usable text, or a background clip the encoder could not read.
